Dr. Stephen Akafo

CEO & Lead Ophthalmologist

Dr. Stephen Akafo (FRCOphth) is a distinguished Ghanaian consultant ophthalmologist and specialist in eye care, retinal surgery, and ophthalmic research.

He serves as a leading consultant at Akafo Eye Services in Accra, Ghana, and has held senior roles, including Head of Ophthalmology at institutions such as Barnor Memorial Hospital.

Professional Qualifications & Leadership

  • Fellowship: Fellow of the Royal College of Ophthalmologists (FRCOphth).
  • Clinical Practice: Consultant Ophthalmologist at Akafo Eye Services.
  • Specializations: General Ophthalmology, Retinal Surgery, Glaucoma Management, and Pediatric Eye Conditions.
  • Academic & Clinical Roles: Formerly associated with the University of Ghana and Korle-Bu Teaching Hospital, contributing to medical education and surgical training across West Africa.

Clinical Focus & Research Contributions

Dr. Akafo has significantly contributed to academic literature and international multi-center studies focused on eye diseases prevalent in West African populations.

Major Research Areas

  • Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma (POAG): Participated in major cross-ancestry genome-wide association studies (GWAS) investigating genetic risk factors and intraocular pressure variations in populations of African origin.
  • Retinal Disorders & Maculopathy: Contributed to clinical evaluations of retinal and choroidal thickness in indigenous Ghanaian populations, as well as landmark investigations into West African Crystalline Maculopathy (WACM).
  • Sickle Cell Retinopathy & Ocular Manifestations: Co-authored research examining the magnitude and patterns of ocular complications in patients with sickle cell disease at Korle-Bu Hospital in Accra.
  • Pediatric Ophthalmology: Investigated early detection, presentation stages, and patient outcomes for retinoblastoma in children.
